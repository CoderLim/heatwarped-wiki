Heatwarped Wiki (“we”, “us”) operates **heatwarped.wiki**, an unofficial fan reference for the game Heatwarped. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you visit the site and how we use it.

## Information we collect

Most wiki content can be read **without creating an account**. Depending on how you use the site, we may process:

1. **Usage and analytics data** — Pages viewed, approximate location (country/region), device type, browser, and referral source. We use this to understand traffic and improve the wiki.
2. **Cookies and similar technologies** — Small files stored on your device. See the Cookie section below.
3. **Contact information** — If you email us, we receive your address and message content.
4. **Account information (optional)** — If you create an account for site features that require sign-in, we store the details you provide (such as name and email) as described in our authentication system.

We do **not** sell personal information.

## Analytics services

We may use privacy-focused or industry-standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics 4 and Plausible) to measure site usage. These services receive technical data about your visit. Where required, we configure them to minimize personal identification.

You can limit tracking with browser extensions, “Do Not Track” settings, or opt-out tools provided by analytics vendors.

## Cookies we use

| Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Essential** | Locale preference, session security for signed-in users |
| **Analytics** | Aggregate visit statistics |
| **Preference** | UI choices such as theme where applicable |

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work correctly if essential cookies are disabled.

## How we use information

We use collected data to:

- Operate and improve the wiki.
- Respond to support or correction requests.
- Detect abuse, spam, or technical issues.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share limited information with:

- **Service providers** (hosting, analytics, email) under confidentiality obligations.
- **Authorities** when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

## Data retention

Analytics data is retained according to each provider’s settings. Support emails are kept as long as needed to resolve your request. Account data is retained while your account is active and deleted or anonymized upon closure, subject to legal requirements.

## Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. Contact us below to exercise these rights.

## Children

This site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us so we can delete it.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
