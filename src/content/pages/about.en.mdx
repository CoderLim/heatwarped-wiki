Heatwarped Wiki is an **unofficial, fan-made reference site** for [Heatwarped](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4846360/Heatwarped/), the open-world arcade street racing game developed and published by Sealime.

We are **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Sealime**, Steam, Valve, or any other rights holder. Game names, logos, and related assets belong to their respective owners and are used here for identification and informational purposes.

## What we publish

This wiki focuses on **verified information** drawn from official sources — primarily the Heatwarped Steam store page, the developer’s public announcements, and what can be confirmed in the public Steam demo. When something has not been announced, we say so instead of filling gaps with speculation.

Our editorial standards are described in the [Source Policy](/source-policy).

## Why this site exists

Heatwarped is a new title, and search results for similar “Heat” names can be confusing. We aim to give players a clear, source-conscious place to check release status, system requirements, demo download steps, gameplay details, and vehicle information without mixing in unverified claims.

## Independence

- Editorial content is written and maintained independently.
- Affiliate or advertising relationships (if any) do not change how we label confirmed vs. unconfirmed facts.
- Corrections are welcome when supported by an official source.
