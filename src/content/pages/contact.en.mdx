## Email

**support@heatwarped.wiki**

We read every message. Typical replies take a few business days; complex rights or privacy requests may take longer.

## What to include

To help us respond quickly, please include:

- **Corrections** — the page URL, what you believe is wrong, and a link to an official source (Steam listing, developer post, etc.).
- **Privacy requests** — the nature of your request (access, correction, deletion) and enough detail for us to locate relevant records.
- **Rights-holder notices** — the specific URL(s), a description of the material, and proof of authority to act for the rights holder.

## Scope

Heatwarped Wiki is an unofficial fan reference. We cannot speak for Sealime, Steam, or Valve, and we cannot provide official support for the game client, purchases, or refunds. For those topics, use Steam Support or the developer’s official channels.
